# The user's job

One user: Islam, a developer and designer at QIC who also studies AI engineering, runs side projects and wants a clear view of their whole life.

## Moments of use

1. **Morning, 2 minutes.** Open the dashboard. Questions: what is on fire, what is due, what should I do first? Must be answerable without clicking.
2. **Between tasks, 30 seconds.** Glance at Next moves, pick the next one, mark the last one done.
3. **Evening, 30 seconds.** Do the daily check-in (sleep, energy, mood, workout, spend).
4. **Weekly, 10 minutes.** Explore: where did my time go, which projects are slipping, how are sleep and learning trending, the life map.

## Decisions the screen must support

- Which item do I act on now? (Needs you, Next moves)
- Am I neglecting an area of my life? (Life map, project-time bars, heatmaps)
- Is my learning streak alive? (Learning tile)
- Is a source broken or not connected? (Status dots in the header, not-connected states)

## Not in scope for v1

Trading, phone access, editing Jira or GitHub from the dashboard, multi-user anything.
